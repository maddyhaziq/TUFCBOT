TUFCBOT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TUFCBOT ("the Bot") handles information when used in Discord servers. TUFCBOT is operated for The Unfiltered Corner community.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, TUFCBOT may process:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message/interaction information needed to operate commands.
  • Discord usernames or display names when necessary to identify members in Bot responses.
  • Gold Pass information entered by authorized server staff, including start and end dates.
  • Information contained in a Google Sheet when a server administrator has configured the Bot to access that sheet.
  • Bot configuration and settings required to operate features in a server.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain TUFCBOT's features, including Gold Pass tracking, Party of the Day notifications, community utilities, server configuration, troubleshooting, and security.

TUFCBOT does not sell personal information.

3. Google Sheets

If a server uses the Google Sheets integration, TUFCBOT may access the spreadsheet selected or authorized by the server administrator. The information processed depends on the content and permissions of that spreadsheet.

Server administrators are responsible for ensuring that information placed in a connected spreadsheet is appropriate and that access to the spreadsheet is properly controlled.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Some information may be stored in TUFCBOT's database or configuration systems for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested features.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved. Server administrators may request deletion of Bot-managed server data by contacting the TUFCBOT developer/administrator.

5. Data Sharing

TUFCBOT does not intentionally sell or publicly disclose stored user information. Information may be processed through third-party infrastructure required to operate the Bot, such as Discord or Google services.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by TUFCBOT. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

TUFCBOT is intended to be used through Discord and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children independently of Discord's own requirements and controls. Users should comply with Discord's age requirements.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact the TUFCBOT developer/administrator regarding questions about information processed by the Bot or requests to delete Bot-managed information, subject to legitimate operational, legal, and security requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TUFCBOT's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the TUFCBOT developer/administrator through the official TUFCBOT or The Unfiltered Corner Discord community.

11. Acceptance

By using TUFCBOT, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
